Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Alderford Homes ("we," "us," or "our") operates alderfordhomes.com and manages Take Me To The River Cabin, Chasing Sunset Cabin, and The WTH Cabin in Sevierville, Tennessee. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

When you contact us, request a quote, or book a stay, we may collect:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • Trip details such as arrival/departure dates and number of guests
  • Payment information, handled directly by our booking and payment processors — we do not store full card numbers ourselves
  • Messages you send us through our website, phone, or text

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to inquiries and process reservations
  • Send booking confirmations, check-in instructions, and stay-related updates
  • Provide customer support before, during, and after your stay
  • Improve our website and guest experience

Text Messaging (SMS)

By providing your mobile phone number, you consent to receive text messages from Alderford Homes related to your inquiry, reservation, or stay (for example, booking confirmations, check-in details, or customer support). Message frequency varies based on your activity. Message and data rates may apply.

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for assistance. Your mobile information will not be shared or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. This applies to all mobile phone numbers collected in connection with SMS consent.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the service providers necessary to run our business and fulfill your reservation — for example, our property management and booking platform (OwnerRez) and payment processors — and only to the extent needed to provide those services.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work with reputable, industry-standard providers for booking and payment processing.

Your Choices

You may contact us at any time to ask what information we have about you, to request corrections, or to request deletion, subject to any records we're required to keep for legal, tax, or reservation purposes.

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
